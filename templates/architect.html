What to do after reviewing this architecture

Review checklist
Questions to ask before implementation
  • Does the architecture support both the reporting and operational needs described?
  • Are governance and access controls sufficient for the data sensitivity involved?
  • Does orchestration include enough observability and retry behavior?
  • Does the proposed cost model fit the expected usage and scale?
