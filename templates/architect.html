IAM Roles & Access Model
🔒 Data Ingestion Role
Write-only to Bronze. No read access to sensitive Gold data.
⚙️ Data Engineer Role
Read Bronze/Silver. Write Silver. Limited Gold access.
📊 Data Analyst Role
Read approved Gold views only. No access to raw PII or Bronze.
🔬 Data Scientist Role
De-identified Silver + approved feature sets. No production PII.
📋 Data Steward Role
Metadata, classification, policy management. No data modification.
👤 Business User Role
Read curated Gold aggregates and certified dashboards only.
Key Security Risks
1
Re-identification risk
customer_id + location + web behaviour can re-identify individuals when combined.
2
Commercial data overexposure
profit, cost_price, and spend may reveal strategic information if Gold access is too broad.
3
Direct Bronze access
Analysts or non-engineering users accessing Bronze could expose raw PII before masking.
4
Permission sprawl
Roles may accumulate excessive access over time without regular quarterly access reviews.

💡 Cost optimisation ideas
📦
Partitioning + columnar formats
Use Parquet or ORC with partition pruning to dramatically reduce Athena and Glue scan costs.
🔁
Incremental loads over full refreshes
Replace nightly full-table reloads with CDC or watermark-based incremental ingestion.
🧊
Archive cold data to Glacier
Move data beyond your retention window to S3 Glacier to cut storage costs by up to 80%.
⚡
Pre-materialise dashboard aggregates
Cache common Gold aggregates to avoid repeated expensive warehouse scans for BI queries.
